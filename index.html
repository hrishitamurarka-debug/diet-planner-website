How to drink enough water

  • Drink a glass of water as soon as you wake up.
  • Drink a glass 30 minutes before each meal.
  • Keep a bottle with you and sip through the day.
  • Drink more when it is hot or after exercise.
  • Pale yellow urine is a sign you are drinking enough.
  • Avoid drinking large amounts just before bed.
